Removed here: pixel-free cover staging and server-lifetime cleanup latch. Retained: authenticated peer/origin/user-session permissions and old-source/input identity; they prevent access or input being transferred to a different user/connection. GPU/audio formats and bounded congestion limits are outside this connection-core change. Unit3 separately removes an ACK-delay-only viewer disconnect. Actual interactive LockApp capture and real cancellation/reconnect remain Root's later checks; this record does not claim every independent restriction has been eliminated.
